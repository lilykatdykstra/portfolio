About Me

Designer, maker, and explorer. Here's a bit more about who I am.

Hey, I'm Lily 👋

I'm a Creative Technology & Design Engineer freshly graduated from CU Boulder, where I also earned a UX Certificate. My work lives at the intersection of design and engineering — the sweet spot where something looks beautiful and actually works.

I grew up in Los Angeles as a competitive swimmer, which taught me early on about discipline, iteration, and getting comfortable being uncomfortable. I studied abroad in Florence, Italy at the Florence University of the Arts and have explored 15+ countries along the way. Travel has shaped how I see design: context, culture, and human behavior are everything.

I care deeply about the intersection of design and technology, and I'm always making something — whether that's a patchwork denim bag, a wire sculpture, a sensor-driven greenhouse, or a UX redesign. I believe the best solutions come from truly understanding people first, then building backward from empathy.

Right now I'm actively networking and looking for UX/product design and creative technology roles where I can bring both my design eye and technical hands to real problems.
