Cheyenne Mountain Zoo Redesign

Overview

A comprehensive redesign of the Cheyenne Mountain Zoo's online ticketing experience, targeting the core friction points that caused visitor frustration and cart abandonment. The project began with extensive user research and culminated in a tested, high-fidelity Figma prototype.

The existing ticketing flow required users to navigate multiple disconnected pages, re-enter information repeatedly, and make decisions without adequate context — leading to confusion, especially for first-time visitors planning complex group trips.

Problem Statement

Zoo visitors — especially families and group organizers — struggled to complete ticket purchases online due to a fragmented, unclear interface. The site's information architecture buried critical details like parking, accessibility options, and add-on experiences, resulting in calls to the zoo's customer service line for clarification.

  • Ticket type selection was confusing — age categories were ambiguous
  • Membership discounts were not surfaced at the right moment in the flow
  • No clear path to add parking, dining, or special experiences
  • Mobile experience was especially broken — key CTAs were below the fold

Research & Discovery

I conducted heuristic evaluations of the existing site, competitive analysis of three other zoo and attraction ticketing systems, and user interviews with five participants who had recently purchased attraction tickets online.

Key insights: users expected a step-by-step wizard-style flow with a persistent progress indicator; they wanted to see total cost update in real time; and they were overwhelmed by too many choices presented simultaneously.

Design Process

I created low-fidelity wireframes to explore the new information architecture, then moved into mid-fidelity flows for usability testing with 4 participants. After two rounds of iteration, I built a polished high-fidelity prototype in Figma using the zoo's existing brand palette.

Key design decisions: a sticky progress bar showing steps (Date → Tickets → Add-Ons → Review → Pay), contextual tooltips explaining ticket categories, and a real-time order summary panel visible throughout checkout.

High-fidelity Figma prototype

Outcome & Result

Usability testing of the redesigned prototype showed a significant improvement in task completion rate — participants completed the checkout flow in under 3 minutes on average, compared to 7+ minutes on the original site. All participants rated the redesigned experience as "easy" or "very easy."

The redesign also surfaced membership upsell opportunities naturally within the flow, with 3 of 4 test participants pausing to consider the membership option — compared to 0 on the original.
